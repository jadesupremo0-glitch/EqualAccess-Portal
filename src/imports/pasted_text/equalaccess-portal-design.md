Design a complete high-fidelity, responsive, accessible web application for a capstone thesis titled:

EqualAccess Portal: A Data-Driven System for Government Benefits for Persons with Disabilities (PWDs)

The system is a centralized web platform for the Persons with Disabilities Affairs Office (PDAO) of Los Baños, Laguna. It aims to help Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) easily access government benefits and programs, submit assistance requests, receive updates, communicate with the PDAO, and explore employment opportunities. It also enables administrators to efficiently manage PWD records, benefits, requests, reports, and users.

The interface should look like a modern government digital service portal, emphasizing accessibility, simplicity, inclusivity, professionalism, and trust.
