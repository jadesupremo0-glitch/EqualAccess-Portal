Figma AI Prompt: PWD Assistance and Benefits Management System

Design a complete, modern, accessible, and responsive web application UI for a PWD Assistance and Benefits Management System in the Philippines.

The system has two separate interfaces:

PWD User Portal
Administrator Portal

The primary goal of the system is to help Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) easily access information about government benefits and programs, submit assistance requests, communicate with administrators, and monitor the status of their requests. The admin portal allows authorized staff to manage PWD records, benefits, assistance requests, reports, and system users.

Overall UI/UX Style

Create a professional, trustworthy, inclusive, and highly accessible design suitable for a government or public service platform.

Use:

Clean and modern layout
Minimalist design
Soft and welcoming visual style
High color contrast
Large readable typography
Clear labels and icons
Large buttons and touch-friendly controls
Simple navigation
Consistent spacing and components
Rounded cards with subtle shadows
Clear status badges
Accessible form design
Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
WCAG-inspired accessibility principles
Avoid overly complicated animations or decorative elements

Use a calm and professional color palette with blue as the primary color, supported by white, light gray, and accessible accent colors for success, warning, and error states.

Use a consistent design system with:

Primary blue
Secondary blue
White
Light gray backgrounds
Dark gray text
Green for approved/success
Orange or yellow for pending/warning
Red for rejected/error

Typography should be modern, highly readable, and accessible. Use a clean sans-serif font such as Inter, Poppins, or similar.

Design the UI with accessibility in mind for users who may have visual, motor, or cognitive disabilities:

High contrast
Large text
Clear hierarchy
Simple language
Large clickable areas
Visible keyboard focus states
Descriptive icons
Do not rely on color alone to communicate status
